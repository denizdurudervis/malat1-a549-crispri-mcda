# Project scope and claim guard

## What this repository supports

- Reproducible in-silico prioritization of a frozen 86-guide candidate set.
- Cell-context-specific integration of A549 TSS, accessibility and expression
  evidence.
- Explicit modeling of efficacy–local-safety trade-offs.
- Stochastic rank acceptability under preference and criterion uncertainty.
- Sensitivity analysis across position, sequence-QC and off-target scoring
  assumptions.

## What it does not support

- Experimental MALAT1 repression efficiency.
- Empirical local transcriptional specificity.
- Empirical genome-wide off-target activity.
- Clinical or therapeutic suitability.
- A claim that the leading candidate is universally optimal.

## Preferred language

Use:

- “in-silico prioritized candidate”
- “robust within the prespecified model family”
- “context-specific prioritization”
- “candidate for experimental validation”
- “trade-off comparator”

Avoid:

- “optimal guide”
- “safest guide”
- “validated repression”
- “experimentally effective”
- “clinically suitable”
