# Human–AI assistance disclosure template

This is a drafting template only. The final wording must follow the target
journal’s current policy.

> Generative AI tools were used to assist with code drafting, debugging,
> documentation organization and language refinement. The study question,
> biological scope, inclusion and exclusion decisions, interpretation of
> evidence, validation rules and final scientific conclusions were reviewed and
> approved by the author. All computational outputs used in the manuscript were
> reproduced through the deposited pipeline and checked against locked
> reference results. The AI system is not listed as an author.

Do not submit this wording automatically. Verify the selected journal’s policy
at the time of submission.
